Your steps move a tiny world

Walkscape: Journey is a cozy pixel-art walking adventure for iPhone. Every real step moves your little walker through a hand-drawn world.

Coming soon to iPhone

How it works

Walk as you normally do. Walkscape reads your steps and walking distance from Apple Health, and your walker follows along: a new zone every kilometre, treasure chests on the trail, relics to collect, and a little companion who grows as you walk together. Every walk counts, from a short stroll to a marathon.

Twenty little zones

From the Sunny Meadow over the Summit to Lighthouse Point, and the endless Wanderer's Road beyond.

Walk together

Friends on one trail, teams that explore giant maps and team worlds, and groups with friendly leaderboards.

Collect and dress up

Mystery Boxes full of outfits, hats and hoodies, accessories for your companion, and seasonal festivals.

Cozy and fair

No punishing streaks, no daily distance caps, and no pay-to-win. Coins come from walking; items only change how things look.
