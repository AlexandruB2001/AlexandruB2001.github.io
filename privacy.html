Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

This policy explains what Walkscape: Journey ("Walkscape", "the app") reads, stores and shares, and the choices you have. Walkscape is made by an independent developer. For any privacy question or request, email walkscapegame@gmail.com.

Apple Health

  • Walkscape reads your daily step count and walking and running distance from Apple Health, only after you allow it. It never writes anything to Apple Health.
  • Only daily totals are used. Individual Health samples never leave your phone.
  • Health data is never used for advertising and is never shared with advertisers.

Location

Walkscape does not use GPS or your location, and has no location permission. Friends and teams compare how far you walked, never where.

On your phone

Your game progress (zones, collections, coins, items, companion, journal), settings, ad limits and notification choices are stored on your phone. A demo mode keeps its own separate progress.

When you sign in

Signing in is optional and uses Sign in with Apple. Walkscape does not ask Apple for your name or email. When signed in, our server (hosted on Supabase) stores:

  • Your username, your walker's look and outfit, and your profile title and frame.
  • Friends, blocks, teams and groups you join, and reactions you send.
  • One row of daily step and distance totals per day, only while "Share progress" is on. Friends, teammates and group members can see these totals. Unless you turn it off in Profile, the Daily Leaderboard can also show your username, walker and that day's distance to all players.
  • A cloud save of your game progress, so you can restore it on a new phone. Only you can read it.
  • Records of coin purchases in the game (Mystery Boxes, companions, team and group creation) and the items you own, so they can't be lost or duplicated.
  • Your phone's push notification token, if you turn on friend and team notifications.

To keep leaderboards fair, the server checks daily totals for physically impossible values (for example, more distance than steps could cover). An unusual day is kept, but may be held off leaderboards and noted for review.

Ads

  • Walkscape shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information, such as your advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location from your IP address and ad interactions, as described in Google's privacy policy.
  • iOS asks before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency). In the EEA and UK, Google's consent form is shown first, and you can change your choice any time in Profile, Ad privacy choices.
  • When you finish an optional rewarded ad while signed in, Google tells our server so the coins can be confirmed. This uses a random account ID, never your Health data, name or friends.

Purchases

Any real-money purchases go through Apple. Walkscape never sees your payment details. When signed in, the App Store transaction (product, dates, refund status) is stored with your account.

Children

Walkscape is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Profile, then Account, then Delete account removes your server account and everything stored with it right away. Deleting the app removes the data on your phone. You can turn off progress sharing and notifications in Profile at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
