Common questions

My steps aren't showing up

Walkscape reads steps and walking distance from Apple Health. Open the Settings app, then Health, Data Access & Devices, Walkscape, and make sure both are allowed. Then pull down to refresh on the Journey tab.

Yesterday's walk looks short

Open the app and give it a moment: Walkscape re-reads the past few days from Apple Health, so walking after your last check of the day is still counted in full.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Sign in with Apple in the Profile tab on both phones. Your progress, coins and items are saved to your account and restored on the new phone.

How do I delete my account?

Profile, then Account, then Delete account. This removes your account and everything stored with it right away.

Does Walkscape track my location?

No. Walkscape never uses GPS or your location. Friends compare how far they walked, never where.
